Our mission

Zawiyah began with a simple passion: making art for others. Every coaster, sign and journal is made by hand to spread a little love, whether it becomes a gift for someone special or a small treat for yourself.

Our mission is to create pieces that bring warmth into your home and give you a quiet corner to slow down, create and come back to yourself.

Behind the name

In Arabic, زاوية (zawiyah) means a corner. To us, it is that cozy corner of the world where handmade things are made with care and shared with love.
